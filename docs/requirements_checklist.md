# Requirements and evidence

This checklist records implemented evidence and data-dependent limits. It does not certify production readiness.

| Brief | Implemented evidence | Remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A: forensics | `data_audit.json`, numeric/categorical distributions, missingness versus training fraud, validity checks, leakage review and treatments | Source provenance and authorization-time lineage cannot be verified from this CSV. |
| B: advanced EDA | 31 analytical plots; eight nonlinear association curves; three labeled interaction heatmaps with cell counts | Hour and weekday are the only time-period fields; no calendar trend or seasonality claim. |
| C: features | 18 derived features; original/engineered comparisons; per-feature definitions; fold-fitted merchant normalization and channel deviation | Population deviation is supported; individual customer-history features require IDs and timestamps. |
| D: models | Logistic, random forest, extra trees; randomized search for each family; reproducible training folds; candidate fold summaries | Stratified folds are not chronological; tuned-candidate CV summaries are descriptive rather than nested. |
| E: cost | Explicit assumptions; joint review/block search; nine sensitivity settings; 0.50 comparison; all-candidate economics and quantitative rank reversals | Costs and intervention effectiveness are hypothetical; optimized development costs are optimistic. |
| F: calibration | Sigmoid/isotonic comparison; raw versus calibrated Brier/log loss, calibration curves, slope, bias and risk bands | 27 calibration-fit frauds and poor final regression-test slope prevent claiming probability reliability. Sigmoid is a regularized logistic map of base probabilities, not conventional unregularized Platt scaling of margins. |
| G: imbalance | Prevalence, class weighting, fit-only undersampling wrapper around preprocessing/model; AP/ROC, fold precision/recall/F1 and policy costs | Resampling conclusions are restricted to the measured settings. |
| H: explanations | Global permutation importance, fold stability, dependence and interaction analysis; five samples; every flagged inference gets signed local terms | Association only. Logistic terms are base-logit contributions, not calibrated-probability contributions. Tree fallback ablations are nonadditive. Customer-facing reason wording requires review. |
| I: operations | Three actions; constrained development thresholds; volumes/capture/false-positive burden/cost; approve-all, binary and review-only baselines; recall/workload sweep | Capacity is a batch fraction, not an implemented queue scheduler or SLA. |
| J: stress | Amount/velocity/missingness/channel/extreme/prevalence probes; corrected missing-input decisions with separate financial exposure; CV variability | Reused labels and artificial inputs do not simulate real future fraud adaptation. |
| K: governance | Model card, 12-page strategy PDF, monitoring metrics/owners, drift reference rules and review/retrain/disable/rollback proposal | Organizational approvals, alerts and production integrations are proposals, not deployed controls. |

The required business questions are answered with measured tables in `reports/assessment_answers.md` and in the notebook. All eight exact numbered deliverables are generated by `scripts/run_analysis.py`.

The test partition was excluded from feature fitting, candidate selection, calibration fitting and policy optimization. It had already been inspected before this revision; the rerun is regression evidence and cannot replace a fresh external or later-period holdout. Obtaining event time, label time, customer IDs, point-in-time feature lineage, actual losses and analyst outcomes remains necessary for stronger validation.
